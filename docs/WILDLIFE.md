# Forest wildlife: wild boar

A playable wildlife increment under USER_SPEC_RU section7, following integration of pears and performance changes. This adds world variety rather than another production chain. Boars spawn naturally in forest/birch/old-growth-birch/dark-forest biomes through a reloadable `reimagined:boar_habitat` tag and ordinary NeoForge biome modifier. Weight4, groups2–3, normal CREATURE population cap, vanilla animal ground/light rules. No universal spawn injector or tick-time world generation. Natural encounters in a fresh survival world still require longer observation; a decoded biome spawn entry is not that evidence.

Adults have20health,4base melee damage,16block follow range and0.25movement. They are neutral until individually attacked, using vanilla hurt-target/melee goals. No herd alert, nearest-target hunter, global ecosystem, migration simulation or new custom tick loop. Young animals flee and cannot deal melee damage even if a target is assigned. Fire/freezing permits vanilla panic; adults otherwise defend themselves. Peaceful players, creative players and spectators are not valid AI targets. Boars do not retaliate against their own species. Target memory/pathfinding remain vanilla and bounded by ordinary follow range; no custom permanent anger system exists.

Feed carrots, beetroot or orchard pears (reloadable `reimagined:boar_food` tag) to lure/breed them using vanilla Animal interaction. Two fed adults create one live piglet and normal6000tick parental cooldown; feeding young animals accelerates growth through vanilla rules. Keep enclosures if breeding near crops; boars do not implement block destruction or crop raiding. No taming/ownership/menu/new feed production is added.

Hunting an adult yields1–3raw pork, with vanilla Looting count increases and cooking when burning; piglets drop no loot. Ordinary pork recipes remain unchanged. Existing local journal observation recognizes the registered species automatically, without a second observer or new guide chapter. Spawn egg is in the ordinary creative egg tab and integrated item browser. EN/RU names are provided.

Original64x64pixel art and original quadruped mesh include a snout, mane, ears, tail and adult-only tusks. Vanilla QuadrupedModel supplies walking/head/baby transforms. Vanilla pig sound events are referenced, not copied. No external mod assets/code or required dependency.
